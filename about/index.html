04 / PRINCIPLES

我怎样做事How I work

  • 先定义证据，再讨论结论。可验证性应该出现在系统设计的第一天。Define evidence first, then discuss conclusions. Verifiability belongs on day one of system design.
  • 让失败可定位。一个系统如果不允许回退和纠错，就没有真正的可靠性。Make failure locatable. A system without fallback and correction is not genuinely reliable.
  • 用作品说话。技能不应该是一个百分比，而应该对应到项目、测试或研究结果。Let the work speak. A skill should map to a project, test or research result, not a percentage.
