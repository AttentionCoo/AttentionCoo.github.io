看见抽象：面向多模态大模型的纯视觉隐喻理解基准Seeing the Abstract: A Benchmark for Visual-Only Metaphor Understanding in Multimodal Large Language Models

Shan Zhao, Zhao Yang, Tianwei Yan, Yusong Gong, Qian Wan, Shizhao Chen, Shezheng Song, Chengyu Wang, Meng Wang

视觉隐喻让人能够仅通过图像理解抽象意义：在两个视觉上不相似的概念之间建立跨域连接，而不依赖语言提示。

我们提出 VisMet-Bench，一个面向单模态视觉隐喻理解的评测基准。它由 754 组精心策划的图像对构成，采用三层、五任务的递进式框架，并在 11 个先进多模态大模型上进行了系统评估。

结果揭示了一个重要差距：当前模型在跨域视觉隐喻推理上仍显著落后于人类水平。这意味着，多模态模型在看见之后，距离真正理解抽象关系还有很长的路。

Visual metaphors let people understand abstract meaning through images alone, connecting concepts across visually dissimilar domains without relying on language prompts.

We propose VisMet-Bench, a benchmark for monomodal visual metaphor understanding. It contains 754 curated image pairs, uses a three-tier, five-task progressive framework, and evaluates 11 advanced multimodal large language models.

The results reveal an important gap: current models still lag significantly behind human-level cross-domain visual metaphor reasoning. Seeing is not yet understanding.

  • 问题：多模态模型能否只通过图像理解抽象概念，而不借助语言解释？Question: can a multimodal model understand abstract meaning from images alone, without a language explanation?
  • 方法：用 754 组图像对和三层五任务框架，把视觉隐喻理解拆解为可评测的能力。Method: use 754 image pairs and a three-tier, five-task framework to turn visual metaphor understanding into measurable capabilities.
  • 意义：把模型从识别物体推进到理解跨域关系，为多模态抽象推理提供可复现的评测基础。Why it matters: move models beyond object recognition toward cross-domain relational understanding, with a reproducible evaluation foundation.
  • 754 组图像对：覆盖不同主题、构图与跨域映射方式。754 image pairs: spanning topics, compositions and cross-domain mappings.
  • 三层递进框架：从基础识别到更高阶的隐喻关系理解。Three-tier framework: from basic recognition to higher-order metaphor understanding.
  • 五类任务：用多种问题形式评估模型的跨域抽象推理能力。Five task families: multiple question forms for cross-domain abstract reasoning.
  • 视觉理解不等于抽象理解：模型能描述图像内容，却不一定能捕捉两个概念之间的关系。Visual recognition is not abstract understanding: models can describe an image without capturing the relation between concepts.
  • 跨域映射仍是难点：当隐喻依赖视觉上不相似的领域时，模型与人类水平存在明显差距。Cross-domain mapping remains hard: when the metaphor relies on visually dissimilar domains, models fall short of human performance.
  • 评测需要过程视角：只统计最终答案会掩盖模型在哪一层推理上失败。Evaluation needs process awareness: final-answer accuracy hides which layer of reasoning failed.
