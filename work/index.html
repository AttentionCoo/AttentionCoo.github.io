02 / PRINCIPLES

我优化什么What I optimize for

  • 可审计性：让结果之外的过程、工具和证据可以被检查。Auditability: make processes, tools and evidence inspectable beyond the answer.
  • 可回退性：复杂编排需要确定性的回退路径，而不是只依赖一次生成。Recoverability: complex orchestration needs deterministic fallbacks, not a single generation.
  • 可验证性：用测试、指标、证据和真实交互证明系统做到了什么。Verifiability: prove what the system does with tests, metrics, evidence and real interactions.
